This chapter introduces a number of new approaches to learning about attention. For instance, people are now able to use "consciousness" and "cross-attention" to help them better understand the world around them. These approaches can also be used to train machine translation. In particular, they can use an encoder to transform an input sequence into continuous representation. At Duke's next hackathon, Sam asks his U.M. to tell him how sick he was two weeks ago. He wants to make sure that everyone knows what is going on without having to worry about trying to extract sentiment from the conversation.
