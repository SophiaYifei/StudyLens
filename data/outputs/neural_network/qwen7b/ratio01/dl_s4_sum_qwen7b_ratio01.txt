### Summary of Lecture on Natural Language Processing (NLP)

#### Introduction to NLP
- **Tasks**: Search, machine translation, spam tagging, sentiment analysis, topic modeling, Q&A, chatbots, text summarization, text generation, multimodal processing.
- **Applications**: Google Translate, Amazon review summaries, chatbot implementations, etc.
- **Challenges**:
  - Homonyms and synonyms
  - Observations not independent (history matters)
  - Semantic ambiguity
  - Slang and colloquialisms
  - Acronyms
  - Variable length sequences
  - Sarcasm and humor

#### Text Preprocessing
- **Pipeline**: Raw text → Tokenization → Stop word removal → Lemmatization/Stemming → Model input
- **Tokenization**: 
  - Word-level: Split text into words (e.g., "I love NLP" → ["I", "love", "NLP"])
  - Subword-level: Learn common subwords (e.g., "unhappiness" → ["un", "happy", "ness"])
  - Character-level: Split into individual characters (e.g., "I love NLP" → ["I", " ", "l", "o", "v", "e", " ", "N", "L", "P"])
- **Stop Words**: Common words with little semantic content (e.g., "the", "is", "a").
- **Lemmatization vs. Stemming**: 
  - **Stemming**: Fast, crude (e.g., "running" → "run"). Produces non-words.
  - **Lemmatization**: Accurate, always produces real words (e.g., "studies" → "study").
