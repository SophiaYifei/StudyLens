### Summary of Lecture on Model Evaluation and Data Preparation

#### Major Topics Covered:

1. **Model Evaluation**
   - **Training & Test Sets**:
     - Goal: Create a model that performs well on unseen data.
     - Split data into Training (80-90%), Validation (10-20%), and Test (10-20%) sets.
     - Importance of keeping the test set untouched until final evaluation.
   
2. **Sampling Techniques**
   - **Nonprobability Sampling**: Convenience, Snowball, Judgment, Quota.
     - Examples: Google Images, Twitter followers, Expert opinions.
     - Risk of bias.
   - **Random Sampling**: Simple random, Stratified, Weighted.
     - Stratified ensures representation of subgroups; Weighted assigns weights to samples.
   
3. **Data Leakage**
   - Definition: Test data influencing training process.
   - Consequences: Over-optimistic performance, poor generalization.
   - Common sources: Feature selection, scaling, imputation, duplicate observations, time series splits.
   - Solutions: Always split data before feature selection, validate on untouched data, use cross-validation.
   
4. **Model Comparison and Selection**
   - Train/validation/test split: 60-80% training, 10-20% validation, 10-20% test.
   - Cross-validation: K-Fold, ShuffleSplit, Leave-One-Out.
   - Bias-variance tradeoff: More complex models can overfit.
   - Recognizing overfitting: Diverging training and validation scores.
   
5. **Evaluation Metrics**
   - Regression: MSE, MAE, MAPE, R²,
