Trend and Seasonality
We can break time series down into two main components:
Trend: the long-term trend of the data
Seasonality: the cyclical fluctuations in the data
Seasonality
Data can have seasonality (periodicity) on multiple scales: hourly, daily, weekly, annually etc.
