This chapter introduces the Narrator, who describes how he uses "tokens" to train machine learning models. He then shows how they can be used as predictors of future events in a cell using two different kinds of probabilities -- the likelihood of continued or changing states, and the probability for an event to occur in the next. The Narrators also discuss other large-event-based approaches that have been used to advance machine learning. In particular, they discuss how they used reinforcement learning to train image recognition systems.
