### Summary of Lecture Material on Introduction to Neural Networks

#### Journey from Neuron to Neural Network

**1943**: Walter Pitts and Warren McCulloch introduced the **simplest neuron model**, a linear function \( f(x, w) = \sum_{i} w_i x_i \).

**1958**: Frank Rosenblatt demonstrated the **Perceptron**, a neuron with a **threshold**:
\[ y = 
\begin{cases}
1, & \text{if } \sum_{i} w_i x_i - T > 0 \\
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\]

**Multilayer Perceptron**: Combining multiple perceptrons to form a **deep network** capable of approximating any function.

#### History of Neural Networks

- **1943-1958**: Early models like McCulloch-Pitts and Perceptron.
- **1982**: Hopfield's bidirectional neural network.
- **1986**: Backpropagation for training neural networks.
- **2009**: ImageNet competition (Fei-Fei Li).
- **2012**: AlexNet (Alex Krizhevsky et al.).

#### When to Use Deep Learning

- **Advantages**: Handles unstructured data, complex relationships, and requires fewer engineered features.
- **Disadvantages**: Computationally expensive, difficult to train, and can be hard to interpret.

#### Activation Functions

- **Role**: Control output range, add nonlinearity, and learn complex patterns.
- **Examples**: Sigmoid, ReLU, Softmax.

#### Training a Neural Network

1. **Initialization**: Randomly initialize weights and biases.
2. **Forward Pass**: Propagate input through network layers to generate output.
3. **Loss Calculation**: Compute error using a chosen loss function (e.g., Mean Squared Error, Cross Entropy).
4. **Backward Pass (Backpropagation)**: Calculate gradients of loss with respect to weights.
5. **Gradient Descent**: Update weights to minimize loss.

#### Optimizers

- **SGD**: Basic gradient descent with fixed learning rate.
- **Momentum**: Smooths training by considering previous weight updates.
- **Adagrad**: Adaptive learning rate for infrequent features.
- **RMSprop**: Adaptive learning rate for sparse data.
- **Adam**: Combination of momentum and adaptive learning rates.

#### Regularization Techniques

- **Early Stopping**: Stop training when validation error starts increasing.
- **Learning Rate Scheduling**: Adjust learning rate dynamically.
- **L1/L2 Regularization**: Add penalties to weights to promote sparsity.
- **Batch Normalization**: Standardize inputs to stabilize training.
- **Dropout**: Randomly drop neurons to prevent overfitting.

#### Challenges in Training Neural Networks

- **Vanishing/Exploding Gradients**
- **Local Minima**
- **Overfitting**
- **Long Training Cycles**
