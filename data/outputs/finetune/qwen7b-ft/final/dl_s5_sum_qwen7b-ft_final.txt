### Summary of Lecture on Natural Language Processing (NLP)

#### Overview
- **Week 7** covers advanced NLP topics including attention mechanisms, transformer architectures, and practical applications like text similarity, summarization, and topic modeling.
- **Key Concepts**: Self-attention, multi-head attention, cross-attention, and the transformer architecture.

---

#### Attention Mechanisms
- **Self-Attention**
  - **Purpose**: Add context to word embeddings.
  - **Process**:
    1. **Dot Product Scores**: Compute scores \( \text{score}(i, j) = v_i \cdot v_j \).
    2. **Softmax Normalization**: Normalize scores to get weights \( w(i, j) = \text{softmax}(\text{score}(i, :))[j] \).
    3. **Weighted Sum**: Combine embeddings using weights \( Y_i = \sum_j w(i, j) \times v_j \).

- **Multi-Head Attention**
  - **Purpose**: Learn different types of attention relationships.
  - **Process**:
    1. **Linear Transformations**: Apply linear transformations to queries, keys, and values.
    2. **Scaled Dot-Product Attention**: Compute attention scores and normalize.
    3. **Concatenation and Linear Transformation**: Concatenate all heads and apply a linear transformation.

- **Cross-Attention**
  - **Purpose**: Attend to another sequence.
  - **Process**:
    1. **Query-Sequence Attention**: Compute attention scores between query and key sequences.
    2. **Normalization**: Normalize scores to get weights.
    3. **Weighted Sum**: Combine values using weights.

---

#### Transformer Architecture
- **Components**:
  - **Encoder**: Processes input sequence through stacked self-attention and feed-forward layers.
  - **Decoder**: Generates output sequence through masked self-attention, cross-attention, and feed-forward layers.
  - **Feed-Forward Layer**: Non-linear transformation of input embeddings.
  - **Layer Norm**: Normalize activations across features.
  - **Positional Encoding**: Adds position information to embeddings.

- **Types of Transformers**:
  - **Encoder-Only**: Used for tasks like sentence classification.
  - **Decoder-Only**: Used for tasks like text generation.
  - **Encoder-Decoder**: Used for tasks like translation and summarization.

---

#### Applied NLP Tasks
- **Text Similarity**
  - **Lexical Similarity**: Similar vocabulary.
  - **Semantic Similarity**: Similar meaning.
  - **Calculation**: Measure similarity between embeddings using cosine similarity.

- **Text Summarization**
  - **Extractive Summarization**: Select sentences from original text.
  - **Abstractive Summarization**: Generate new text retaining key points.
  - **Models**: BART, T5, Pegasus.

- **Topic Modeling**
  - **Approaches**: Supervised (LDA, LSA), Unsupervised (BERTopic).
  - **Objective**: Tag documents based on topics or attributes.

### Key Formulas
- **Self-Attention Score**: \( \text{score}(i, j) = v_i \cdot v_j \)
- **Self-Attention Weight**: \( w(i, j) = \text{softmax}(\text{score}(i, :))[j] \)
- **Self-Attention Output**: \( Y_i = \sum_j w(i, j) \times v_j \)
- **Scaled Dot-Product Attention**: \( \text{Attention}(Q, K, V) = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) @ V \)
- **Multi-Head Attention**: \( \text{MultiHead}(Q, K, V) = \text{Concat}(\text{head}_1, ..., \text{head}_H) @ W_O \)
- **TF-IDF**: \( \text{tf-idf}(t, d) = \text{tf}(t, d) \times \log\left(\frac{N}{df(t)}\right) \)
- **Cosine Similarity**: \( \text{sim}(A, B) = \frac{(A \cdot B)}{\|A\| \times \|B\|} \)
- **ROUGE-L**: Based on LCS length and LCS ratio.
