Neurons in a convolutional layer are connected only to nearby neurons from the previous layer (rather than all neurons like FC layers)
They represent a narrower range of features / small patches of the image
Useful when we expect most of the information to be local
They will not be affected by objects elsewhere
Because we use the same weights for patches across the image, it gives us the property of translation invariance
Regardless of where the object is in the image, the network can find it
