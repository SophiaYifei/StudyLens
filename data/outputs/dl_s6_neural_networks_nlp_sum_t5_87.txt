The Narrator discusses the most important applications and approaches in the field of natural language processing. He explains two approaches: alliteration and Markov chains. These approaches work well because they take into account how words are entrusted to the human being, but they do not capture all the challenges that might arise when using conventional methods. In Chapter 2, the Narrators describes his machine learning project. This chapter introduces several ways to reduce the size of their machine learning system while still achieving the same end goal.
