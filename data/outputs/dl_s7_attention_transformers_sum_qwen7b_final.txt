### Summary of Lecture Material

#### 1. **Tokenization**
   - **Pre-trained Model Tokenizers**: Use specific tokenizers provided by models like BERT or GPT for consistent tokenization.

#### 2. **Stop Word Removal**
   - **Purpose**: Remove common words that contribute little to the understanding of sentences/documents.
   - **Example**: Removing "the", "is", "a", "of", "and" from texts.

#### 3. **Term Frequency-Inverse Document Frequency (TF-IDF)**
   - **Definition**: Combines term frequency (how often a word appears in a document) with inverse document frequency (how rare the word is across all documents).
   - **Formula**:
     \[
     \text{tf-idf}(t, d) = \text{tf}(t, d) \times \log\left(\frac{\text{N}}{\text{df}(t)}\right)
     \]
     - \(\text{tf}(t, d)\): Term frequency of term \(t\) in document \(d\).
     - \(\text{df}(t)\): Number of documents containing term \(t\).
     - \(\text{N}\): Total number of documents.

#### 4. **Hidden Markov Models (HMM)**
   - **Combining Transition and Emission Probabilities**: Estimate the most likely hidden state and sequence using both transition probabilities (likelihood of state changes) and emission probabilities (data sources determining hidden state).

#### 5. **Neural Network Architectures**
   - **LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory)**: 
     - **Forget Gate**: Decide what to discard from the cell state.
     - **Input Gate**: Decide what new information to add to the cell state.
     - **Update Cell State**: Combine discarded and new information.
     - **Output Gate**: Generate the final output using the cell state.
   - **Transformer Architecture**:
     - **Self-Attention**: Improves embeddings with context.
     - **Multi-Head Attention**: Runs multiple parallel attention mechanisms.
     - **Cross-Attention**: Attends to another sequence.
     - **Feed-Forward Networks**: Process each token independently.
     - **Residual Connections**: Add input to output to aid gradient flow.
     - **Layer Normalization**: Normalize across feature dimensions.

#### 6. **Fine-Tuning Strategies**
   - **Full Fine-Tuning**: Update all model parameters.
   - **Frozen Backbone Training**: Keep pretrained layers fixed and only train task-specific layers.
   - **Gradual Unfreezing**: Train the final layer first, then progressively unfreeze earlier layers.

#### 7. **NLP Libraries**
   - **NLTK**: Traditional NLP tools.
   - **spaCy**: Efficient industrial-grade NLP pipelines.
   - **Hugging Face Transformers**: Offers pretrained state-of-the-art transformer models for tasks like classification, generation, and summarization.

#### 8. **Applications of NLP**
   - **Text Similarity**: Measures lexical and semantic similarities.
   - **Text Summarization**: Extracts key information from longer texts.
   - **Topic Modeling**: Identifies themes in collections of documents.

#### 9. **Attention Mechanisms**
   - **Self-Attention**: Adds context to embeddings.
   - **Multi-Head Attention**: Runs multiple parallel attention mechanisms.
   - **Cross-Attention**: Attends to another sequence.
   - **Transformer Architecture**: Combines self-attention, feed-forward networks, and residual connections.

#### 10. **Transformer Variants**
   - **Vision Transformers (ViTs)**: Treat image patches as tokens for image recognition.
   - **Mixture of Experts**: Uses a router to activate a subset of parameters for each input token.

#### 11. **Evaluation Metrics**
   - **ROUGE**: Compares generated summaries
