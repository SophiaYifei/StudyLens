Trend and Seasonality
	•	Trend = long-term movement
	•	Seasonality = cyclical patterns
	•	Can exist simultaneously
	•	Trend may increase or decrease
	•	Seasonality may repeat hourly, daily, weekly, yearly
Examples
	•	Temperature
	•	Power demand
	•	Retail / bookstore sales

Time Series Modeling
	•	Standard regression assumes independence
	•	Time series violates independence
	•	Present correlated with past
	•	Autocorrelation key concept
	•	Must model time dependence explicitly
	•	Methods include ARIMA, exponential smoothing

Time Series Decomposition
	•	Break into components:
	◦	Trend
	◦	Seasonality
	◦	Residual
Two models
Additive
	•	Y = Trend + Seasonality + Residual
	•	Linear trend
	•	Constant seasonal amplitude
Multiplicative
	•	Y = Trend × Seasonality × Residual
	•	Non-linear trend
	•	Seasonal amplitude grows with trend
Question
	•	If seasonal swings get larger as values increase, which model fits better?

Autocorrelation
	•	Correlation with lagged version of itself
	•	Measured with Pearson correlation
	•	Durbin-Watson test
	•	Indicator of seasonality
	•	Lag length matters

Markov Processes
	•	Full autocorrelation at lag 1
	•	Current state depends only on previous state
	•	Transition matrix defines probabilities
	•	Used in science, finance, ranking algorithms
